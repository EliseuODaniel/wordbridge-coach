=============================

Paper Details:
--------------
- Title: OpenSubtitles2016: Extracting Large Parallel Corpora from Movie and TV Subtitles
- Authors: Pierre Lison and Jörg Tiedemann
- Conference: LREC 2016 (10th International Conference on Language Resources and Evaluation)
- Year: 2016
- Pages: 923-931
- URL: http://www.lrec-conf.org/proceedings/lrec2016/pdf/854_Paper.pdf

Dataset Summary:
----------------
- Source: OpenSubtitles (http://www.opensubtitles.org/)
- Year: 2016
- Size: ~2 billion words
- Content: Movie and TV subtitles in multiple languages
- Processing: Word frequency lists generated by Hermit Dave
- Format: rank word frequency part_of_speech
- License: Data freely available for research (see OpenSubterms terms)

Project Usage:
--------------
This project (FillTheWord) uses the top 10,000 most frequent English words
from the OpenSubtitles2016 corpus as processed by the FrequencyWords project.

The data was obtained from:
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/raw/master/content/2016/en/en_50k.txt

Only the top 10,000 lines were extracted and stored in en_top_10000.txt.

Notes:
------
- The OpenSubtitles2016 corpus is one of the largest collections of subtitles
- The English portion contains contemporary spoken language from movies and TV shows
- Word frequencies reflect real-world usage in entertainment media
- This makes the data particularly suitable for language learning applications
